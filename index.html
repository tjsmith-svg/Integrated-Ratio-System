How to set up & connect the scales
  1. Open in Bluefy (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) with Bluetooth ON; allow Bluetooth when asked.
  2. Turn the scale on — hold its power button until the display lights up. Turn the cordless remote off.
  3. Tap Connect A. A Bluetooth list pops up — tap the scale named LMC-210, then Connect.
  4. Do the same with Connect B for the second scale. Connect them one at a time so each lands on the right side.
  5. Keep the phone within a few feet of the scales for the strongest signal.

Help

1. Phone setup (do this first)
  • Use the Bluefy browser on iPhone/iPad, or Chrome on Android.
  • Turn the phone's Bluetooth ON, and allow the browser to use Bluetooth when it asks.
  • Keep the screen on and this page in front — Bluetooth and the timer both pause if you switch apps or the screen sleeps.
2. Turn on & connect the scales
  1. Turn the scale on — hold its power button until the display lights up. Turn the cordless remote off (a scale talks to only one thing at a time).
  2. In the app, tap Connect A. A Bluetooth list pops up — tap the scale named LMC-210, then Connect/Pair.
  3. Repeat with Connect B for the second scale. Always connect one at a time so you know which scale is A and which is B (both show the same name).
  4. Keep the phone within a few feet of the scales the whole job. If a scale drops, the app reconnects on its own.

The pressure sensors are optional — one per hose. Connect Sensor A and Sensor B (one at a time) to drive timing from measured PSI instead of vessel weight. Both are logged to the CSV.

What drives the timer

Best source available, in order: pressure sensor → average of both scales → a single scale → manual. The tempo is set at the top of each count (when it shows 20) and held steady through that count.

One scale works too — timing and the weight/percent readout run on a single scale; the ratio check just needs both.

Weight & percent full

Each vessel shows its weight and how full it is (100% = 53 lb, empty = 14.9 lb). The empty figure is approximate and shifts a bit with the hose weight and other factors, so treat the percent as a rough guide, not an exact number.

The A:B ratio

Tap Start to set the baseline. The app tracks the total each vessel has dispensed and shows the A:B ratio, which should stay near 1.00. It only alarms if the ratio stays off for a sustained stretch — a brief hose tug won't trip it, but a closed valve or clog will.

Warnings
  • Warnings show on the banner and beep only when the count reaches the bottom, so they never throw off a shot.
  • Scales not updating: if the feed stops while running, the count keeps going on the last pressure and warns you it isn't live.
Saving data (CSV)

Weights are logged during a job. Tap Download data (CSV) to save or share the log. On a phone it opens the share sheet (save to Files, email, etc.); on a computer it downloads to your Downloads folder. Download before closing the page — the log isn't kept after that.

Job-site troubleshooting (foam, gun, fill, safety)

Field problems and fixes — not app settings.

Gun, nozzle, hose & pressure
A or B side not dispensingCross-contamination — gun left on the ground with the nozzle on, or not cleaned. Fix: clean the needle valves and gun cup, scraping crud with a screwdriver. If that fails, flush the whip: close gun valves → dispense residual into garbage → disconnect whip → add SurfX to fill whip → reconnect → open valves → sit 15 min → dispense into a garbage bag until both sides run clear.
Bubbles in the whipMaterial is out or the line is clogged. Fix: refill/replace material or clear the clog and flush the whip (see above).
Material dribbling out of the gunFlow-rate lever is vertical. Fix: turn the lever to horizontal.
Gun drips foam after releasing the triggerOlder needle valves can't handle a fresh cylinder's high pressure. Fix: on the first few shots from a new cylinder, shut the gun valves right after each shot.
Foam leaks out of the nozzle bottomGasket missing → replace the gasket. Cracked cup around the needle valves → replace the gun. Loose nozzle from an old gasket → replace the gasket and tighten the nozzle until it clicks.
Nozzle hard to twist onto the gunOld, hardened gasket. Fix: replace the gun gasket.
Valve leakingGun was dropped and the valve body cracked. Fix: swap in the backup hose and send the gun for repair.
Pressure not droppingValves closed or defective gun/hose. Fix: check all valves are open → do a clearing shot and look for a clear stream → if spitting, clean all components and replace the hose with the backup.
Fill quality — over/underfill & ratio
OverfillHot metal studs → don't shoot if studs are over 90°F. Baseboard heat on → turn heat off first thing entering a room.
Overfill — consistent (same every cavity)Unidentified obstruction. Fix: assume blocking; shoot under it and 16" over the suspected blocking.
Overfill — inconsistentUnidentified obstruction. Fix: shoot more, shorter body shots — around 20".
UnderfillPressure not updated after 8 cavities → always update pressure after 8 cavities. No supervisor QC → add random QC checks.
Overfill / underfillImproper calibration → shoot a full bottom lift and base calibration on multiple cavities. Variable cavity depth → expect multiple void-filling shots; don't compensate by overfilling.
Off-ratio — residual A or B left overAging blowing agent causes variable flow. Fix: close the heavier side's cylinder valve one full turn at a time until ending weights match, or use flow restrictors.
Calibration
Overfill while calibratingCalibrated under a window or other restricted cavity. Fix: always calibrate in an unrestricted cavity. Use a 7 count for a standard cavity, 2 count for a shallow one.
Improper calibration (over/underfill)Fix: shoot an entire bottom lift and base the calibration on multiple cavities.
Cavity & shooting technique
BlowbackShooting into a stud, fireblock, or bunched fiberglass. Fix: always spritz first; drill a new hole nearby and fill from there.
Can't find the studsWire lath. Fix: estimate the cavity center 8" from the wall corner, mark remaining cavities 16" apart from there; shoot test shots in the first lift to validate.
Horizontal top not "tombstone" shapedBlocking in the cavity. Fix: fill 6–12" below the blocking, then a finishing shot to complete; shoot 16–18" above the blocking for the top of the wall.
Variable-depth cavityBasement wall (CMU bottom, standard top) → calibrate bottom and top separately; shoot bottom in the first 2 lifts, top in the second 2. Older solid-masonry wall → shoot normally but expect more voids.
Foam sprayed on the wallNozzle not securely attached → replace gasket, tighten until it clicks. Bent needle breaking off → never bend the needle when inserting it in the hole.
Material not showing on IR
Drilled through the sheathing into a ventilation channelFix: apply minimal pressure when drilling through fiberglass.
Double-layer drywall (shallow cavity)Fix: use 2 counts on a standard-width cavity, 1 count on narrower cavities.
Only one component shot into the wallFix: remove the wall surface, remove the single component, and repair the wall.
Jobsite, tenant property & safety
SmokeHeat left on — heaters melting the drape. Fix: remove the drape immediately, turn off the heat, let it cool. Always turn heat off when entering a room.
Foam in an outletCover not removed, or outlet draped. Fix: turn off the breaker → chip foam out with a plastic scraper → clean the contacts with a screwdriver.
Foam tracks on the floorFoam on the bottom of a shoe. Fix: cover shoe bottoms with masking tape.
Broken tenant objectsHose not lying flat, or tools set on furniture. Fix: apologize, replace if possible, otherwise pay double the value. Keep the hose flat; never set tools on furniture.
Tape falls off the wallNo-stick paint. Fix: tape just before shooting.
Items lostKeep tools and phone only in the caddy-bag pockets or on window sills.
